🪞Money Mirror

Welcome! 👋

Money Mirror shows where your money goes — forgotten subscriptions, price increases, double charges, and what you really spend each month. It reads the CSV file your bank already lets you download. No bank login. Nothing uploaded anywhere.

One quick question: do you want AI help?

AI is optional here. It only helps name unusual merchants and understand tricky questions. It never sees your amounts or dates.

You can change this any time in ⚙️ Settings.

Where do I find the CSV at my bank?
  • Most banks: Accounts → pick the account → Transactions or Activity → "Download" / "Export" → choose CSV (sometimes called "Excel/Spreadsheet" or "Comma separated").
  • Credit cards: Statements & Activity → "Download transactions" → CSV. Pick the longest date range they allow (often 12–24 months).
  • Can't find it? Search the help pages for "download transactions CSV your bank name".

❔ How to use Money Mirror

  1. Download a CSV from your bank. On your bank's website: Transactions → Download/Export → CSV. Pick the longest date range you can.
  2. Drop it into Money Mirror. Add as many files as you like (checking, credit cards). Re-adding the same file is safe — nothing is counted twice.
  3. Look at the tabs. Overview = the big picture. Subscriptions = things charging you regularly. Alerts = double charges, price increases, fees. Transactions = everything, searchable. Ask = plain-English questions.
  4. Fix a category in the Transactions tab and the app remembers it.

Is it private?

Yes. Your file is read on this computer and stored in a small database in your home folder. Nothing is uploaded. If you turn on a cloud AI, only merchant names and your typed questions are sent — never amounts or dates.

Something not working?

In a terminal, run python scripts/selfcheck.py. The file docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md in the app folder lists every error message with its fix.
